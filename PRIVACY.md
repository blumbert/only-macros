# Privacy Policy — Only Macros

_Last updated: 22 August 2026_

## Short version

Only Macros does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There is no account,
no analytics, no advertising, and no network connection of any kind.

## What the app stores

The macro entries you type — carbohydrates, protein and fat, and the date each
entry was made — are saved in the app's own storage on your device. That is the
only data the app records.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. The app contains no networking code. Your entries never leave your
phone, are never sent to a server, and are not visible to the developer or to
anyone else.

Your data may be included in an iCloud or iTunes backup of your phone if you
have device backups enabled. That backup is controlled entirely by Apple and by
your own device settings; the developer has no access to it.

## Third parties

None. The app includes no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no crash reporting,
and no social or tracking integrations.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its stored data from your device permanently.
There is no copy anywhere else to delete.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this same
address with a new date at the top.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the address listed on the App Store
listing for Only Macros.
